Sign off each commit to confirm this (`git commit -s`), which adds a line in the form of
the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

Pull requests without a sign-off, or that change the licence of existing files, are not
merged. Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix.
